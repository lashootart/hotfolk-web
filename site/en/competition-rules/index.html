Competition rules – Model of the month

Valid from 14 November 2026.

1. Organiser

The organiser is HOTFOLK s.r.o., Nábrežná 4, 039 01 Turčianske Teplice, IČO: 56 323 735, Slovakia (the “organiser”). Contact: admin@hotfolk.sk.

2. Duration

The competition runs in monthly rounds. The start and end of each round are shown on the Model of the month page at hotfolk.sk.

3. Who can enter

Individuals aged 18 or over with a delivery address in Slovakia or Czechia.

Employees and partners of the organiser and their close relatives are excluded.

4. How to enter

Entrants vote for one of three models on the Model of the month page and provide their name, e-mail and agreement to these rules.

Each e-mail address may vote only once per round. Entry is free and no purchase is necessary.

The chance of winning does not depend on which model the entrant voted for.

5. Prize and draw

Each round has one prize: a HOTFOLK 2027 wall calendar of the winner's choice.

The organiser draws the winner at random from all valid votes of the round within 3 days of its end and notifies them by e-mail.

If the winner does not reply with a delivery address within 14 days, the prize is forfeited and a substitute may be drawn.

The prize is delivered to an address in Slovakia or Czechia at the organiser's expense. No cash alternative is offered.

6. Exclusion

The organiser may exclude votes and entrants who break the rules, for example by using fake or automatically generated e-mail addresses or otherwise manipulating the vote.

7. Personal data

The organiser is the data controller. We process your name and e-mail to run the vote, the draw and the prize delivery; for the winner, also the delivery address.

The legal basis is the performance of the competition under these rules. Data is kept for no more than 3 months after the round ends; the winner's data for as long as needed to deliver the prize and meet legal obligations.

Data is stored with Google (Google Sheets). Entrants have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and to complain to a supervisory authority. Send requests to admin@hotfolk.sk.

The winner's first name and surname initial may be published on hotfolk.sk and on social media.

8. Final provisions

The organiser may change these rules or end the competition; changes will be published on this page.

This competition is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Meta, Instagram or Google.
