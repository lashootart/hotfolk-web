About us

A family brand with the countryside at heart.

HOTFOLK® started with a simple idea: to show women and men from Slovakia and Czechia where we feel best – in the countryside. With humour, courage and no pretence.

Our story

The countryside isn't boring. Log houses, orchards, farmyards and meadows have an atmosphere of their own – and when you put people in them who aren't shy, you get something that pleases both the eye and the soul.

We started with calendars; today we also make swimwear, bags, socks and other accessories. We shoot everything ourselves in real places and, wherever we can, we produce in Slovakia.

Behind the brand is a family and a few people who believe sensuality can come with a smile.

What we stand for

Real people

Our photos show real women and men from Slovakia and Czechia – no AI-generated models.

Real places

We shoot in the countryside, in villages and in nature. No studio backdrops pretending to be a meadow.

Made in Slovakia

Swimwear, accessories and calendars are made here, with local partners.
